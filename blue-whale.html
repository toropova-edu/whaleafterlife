The blue whale after death

The story, step by step

Blue whale

Meet the blue whale.

The biggest animal that has ever lived. Her long life has ended, and the sea is carrying her.

Doesn't sink

She puffs up like a balloon.

Gases make her body swell, so she floats for a long time instead of sinking.

A floating feast

Guests from the sky and the sea.

Gulls, an eagle and a raven land on her. Sharks and fish gather underneath. For them, she is a banquet.

Nothing is wasted

She feeds the whole sea.

Everything she leaves behind becomes a meal for someone. Even the biggest goodbye feeds many.

Washed ashore

The waves bring her to Alaska.

She comes to rest on a quiet pebble beach, under snowy mountains.

Curious visitors

People come to see her.

Not everyone gets to meet a whale this big. They bring binoculars, cameras and warm coats.

A careful crew

She cannot stay on the beach.

So a careful crew takes her away piece by piece and recycles what they can.

The beach is quiet again

Nothing was wasted.

People remember the day they saw the biggest animal on Earth, and the beach is clean and calm.
