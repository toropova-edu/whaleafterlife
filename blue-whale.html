Meet the blue whale.

The biggest animal that has ever lived. Her long life has ended, and the sea is carrying her.

Doesn't sink

She puffs up like a balloon.

Gases make her body swell, so she floats for a long time instead of sinking.

A floating feast

Guests from the sky and the sea.

Gulls, an eagle and a raven land on her. Sharks and fish gather underneath. For them, she is a banquet.

Nothing is wasted

She feeds the whole sea.

Everything she leaves behind becomes a meal for someone. Even the biggest goodbye feeds many.

The beach is quiet again
