The orca after death

The story, step by step

Orca

Meet the fish-eating orca.

This is the fish-eating (resident) ecotype of orca. Females typically live about 50 years, and some reach 80 or even 90. This one is very old.

The matriarch

It leads the whole family.

Resident orcas live in families led by an old female. Her children and grandchildren stay with her all their lives. Now its long life is ending.

A long life

It raised a new generation.

It led the family to the best salmon for decades, and it lived to see its grandchildren grow up. Now it slows down, and the whole family stays close.

The farewell

Nobody leaves its side.

Sons, daughters and grandchildren gather around and swim slowly together. They say goodbye in their own way, and the water is very quiet.

A new matriarch

The daughter takes the lead.

The family is not alone. Its oldest daughter becomes the new matriarch, and she leads everyone onward, just as she was taught.

A mystery

Nobody knows how it died.

One summer, an orca is found on the shore of the Solovetsky Islands, in the White Sea. Its teeth are worn down, so it was most likely an old female.

Locals

Fishermen and mushroom pickers.

People who live nearby come across it on their way. Not many orcas visit the White Sea, so everyone stops to look.

For science

The photographer takes samples.

The locals bring a photographer, who photographs the orca and takes samples for scientists. Later, the samples are studied in a lab.

Souvenirs

And then, the teeth…

Some of the locals probably took a tooth or two as a keepsake.
